# Kinferry pitch outline

1. **Problem:** Cross-border transfers fail at identity, address ownership, stale rates, and retries.
2. **Insight:** The agent should draft intent but never own authority.
3. **Product:** One guarded route for plain-language, manual, and recurring family transfers.
4. **Demo:** Verify recipient, prepare quote, trigger a blocked unverified send, then settle a verified devnet send.
5. **Why Solana:** Fast finality, low transaction cost, and programmable owner-controlled policy accounts.
6. **Market:** Infrastructure for licensed remittance operators, wallets, global payroll, and stablecoin applications.
7. **Model:** Platform subscription plus usage-based guard execution.
8. **Moat:** Auditable evidence graph and corridor-specific safety policies.
9. **Roadmap:** audited token program, licensed ramp partner, compliance integrations, pilot corridor.
10. **Ask:** design partners and security reviewers for a regulated pilot.
